# Third-Party Asset Notice

**Create: Mechanical Drive** does not redistribute texture PNG files copied from **Create**, **Create: Aeronautics**, **Simulated**, **Offroad**, or **Sable**.

Some models and rendering definitions reference resource locations provided by these dependency mods. Those resources are resolved by Minecraft at runtime from the user's installed mod files and are not bundled inside the `assets/mechanical_drive` namespace unless explicitly stated otherwise.

---

## Offroad-Derived Model

Create: Mechanical Drive includes a modified model derived from the wheel mount model used by **Offroad**, distributed as part of the **Create: Aeronautics / Simulated** ecosystem.

Permission to use and modify this model in Create: Mechanical Drive was granted directly by the developer of Offroad.

The textures used by this modified model are **not bundled** with Create: Mechanical Drive. Instead, the model continues to reference the corresponding Offroad resource locations at runtime.

As a result, **Offroad remains the source and copyright holder of those texture assets**.
